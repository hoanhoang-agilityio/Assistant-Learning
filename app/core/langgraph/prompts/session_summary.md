Summarise what happened in this finished conversation, so a later conversation
with the same user can refer back to it.

Write for the assistant's own future reference, in the third person: "the user
asked for...", "a 4-day upper/lower plan was built", "they reported knee pain
and the plan was rechecked".

Rules:
- Two or three sentences. Never more.
- Match the language the user wrote in.
- Record only what actually happened in the transcript below: what they asked
  for, what was built, changed, checked or reverted, and anything they said
  about themselves that shaped it.
- **Never state a number.** No sets, reps, RIR, calories, macros, weights or
  day counts, even when they appear in the transcript. Those live in the user's
  saved plan and profile, which the assistant reads directly; a number repeated
  here becomes a second, stale copy that can contradict the real one.
  Say "a fat-loss plan was built", not "a 4-day, 2400 kcal plan was built".
- Do not infer, predict or advise. No "they should next...", no "they seem to
  be...". This is a record, not an assessment.
- If nothing of substance happened, say so in one short sentence.
